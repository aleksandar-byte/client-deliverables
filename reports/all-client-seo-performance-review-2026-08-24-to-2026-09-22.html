What this review covers

Winners in traffic: organic users increased by at least 8%. Winners in quotable leads: quotable SEO / Local leads increased by more than 5%. Losers: a metric declined. Data issues: missing data or an unusual change to check. Notices: changes with too little prior data to draw a firm conclusion.

Winner filters use the total organic users and quotable SEO / Local lead counts. Missing or flagged source data is excluded. A previous value of zero is excluded from these filters because a percentage increase cannot be calculated. Small prior counts are labeled in the findings. These labels describe the measured changes. They do not establish why a change happened. A client can have both gains and declines.

Coverage and limitations
