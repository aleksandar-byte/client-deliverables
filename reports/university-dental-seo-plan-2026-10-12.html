SEO implementation plan

Objective: improve implant search visibility and qualified consultation requests.

Overview of the plan

  1. Website updates. Revise the homepage, implant hub and navigation; consolidate overlapping content.
  2. Content and links. Create 10 implant articles per month, three patient case studies and five relevant backlinks.
  3. Local and Spanish pages. Update Areas Served, prepare up to two approved neighborhood pages and translate the homepage, implant hub and financing page.
  4. Schedule and client inputs. Implement over eight weeks and measure results for six months. Obtain client materials and approvals before dependent releases.
