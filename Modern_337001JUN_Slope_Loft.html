Layout changes and areas

One U-shaped stair core connects the partial basement, living floor and roof office. The 15.1 m² office sits above the living/dining wing, with a raised sloping roof. The other roof planes remain sloped. Bedrooms are retained.

The island is removed to give the six-seat dining table and kitchen working aisle space. Chairs are shown tucked. The main-floor estimate is 150.1 m²; the new upper exterior footprint is 30.3 m² including stairs and walls; the basement exterior footprint is 32.4 m². The kitchen has about 1.85 m to the tucked dining chairs; allowing 0.60 m chair pullback leaves about 1.25 m. Main circulation passes around the table end. These additions exceed the earlier 151 m² cap. These differing area bases must not be presented as certified usable area.

Basement: 26.95 m² inside wall faces, including the approximately 9.14 m² stair/core envelope. About 17.8 m² remains outside that envelope before storage and circulation. Office: 15.12 m² clear rectangle. Deck: approximately 42 m².

Stairs, kitchen and design limits

Study levels −2.85 / 0 / +2.85 m. Each storey has 16 risers of 178 mm, two flights with 280 mm goings and nominal 1 m tread widths. Floor openings align vertically. Sampled 3D stair/landing headroom is at least 2.60 m. Review measurements. This is a spatial concept, not a structural or local building-code approval. Concrete stair supports, reinforcement, fire protection, roof junctions, waterproofing and final guarding require design.
