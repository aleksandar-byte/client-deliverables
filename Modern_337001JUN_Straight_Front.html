Placement, terrain and construction limits

Plot outlines, equal-area split and map alignment remain provisional. The same 109-point survey TIN is used inside its coverage; distant terrain and neighbouring buildings are approximate. A new level platform and graded entrance path are conceptual proposals, not measured existing earthworks or a foundation/retaining-wall design. Numeric clearances are not legal setback approval.

Room proportions and openings are traced from the public drawing, calibrated to its 46-foot width. Roof geometry is inferred from the published ceiling heights and images. This visualization is not a reinforced-concrete structural conversion; local engineering, wall build-ups, drainage and slope stability remain to be designed.
