How to read this

Numbers match the lines in the view. Green marks a measured gap; amber marks space shared during use; red marks a pinch point or overlap. Switch to Walk to try the route.

Shaded areas are movement or standing allowances. Measurements come from this provisional model and are rounded to centimetres; they are not construction dimensions or compliance checks.

Guidance: NKBA kitchen planning. Appliance example: Bosch dishwasher dimensions. No appliance has been selected for purchase.

Walkthrough notes

Measurements still to confirm
