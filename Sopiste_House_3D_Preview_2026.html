Walkthrough notes

Walk between rooms and use the stairs, or choose a room from “Go to.” On a computer, use WASD to move and the arrow keys to turn. Drag to look up, down or around.

The view uses a 1.65 m eye height and a simplified 48 cm wide, 1.80 m tall person. Walls, furniture, glass, low overhead obstacles and unsupported floor edges block movement. This is a navigation study, not a clearance certification.

Room doors are shown open. The linen cupboard is closed: its open door would leave only about 27 cm past the leaf in the upstairs hall. The upper ceiling is a provisional flat 2.80 m height.

What was corrected
  • Opened the hall bathroom from the landing and removed the wall that blocked its entrance.
  • Moved the main walk-in door to its source position, clear of the hanging run.
  • Rebuilt the stair with 17 equal rises, a full-width turning landing and an aligned upper floor opening. Storage walls now stop below the stairs.
  • Connected the recessed patio to its slider and corrected the outer wall envelope.
  • Restored the three toilets, sinks and separate entry cupboard/pantry. The sofa now faces the fireplace.
  • Added legible furniture, door swings, floor labels and a true view from above. Chairs can move 50 cm back from their everyday position.
Measurements still to confirm

This remains a reference study. Wall thicknesses, opening sizes, floor heights and the stair section are inferred. The model checks clear geometry; it does not certify construction dimensions or local compliance.

Stair study: 17 rises of about 179 mm, 280/260 mm goings, 960 mm width and a 960 × 980 mm landing. Calculated headroom below the front slab is about 2.20 m; full headroom, structure, handrails and landings still need a dimensioned section.

The main walk-in keeps one 1.30 m hanging run with about 0.96 m of aisle. Bedroom 3 keeps the 1.20 × 2.00 m mattress; its full frame leaves about 0.95/0.85 m at the sides and 1.01 m at the foot. Its desk remains undecided.
