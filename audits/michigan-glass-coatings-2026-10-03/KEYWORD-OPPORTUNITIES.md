# Michigan Glass Coatings — bounded keyword opportunities

Research date: 3 October 2026. Market: US; architectural residential/commercial window film in Michigan. Automotive-tint demand is deliberately excluded from the opportunity totals. These are planning candidates, not instructions to publish additional pages.

## Method and cost

Ahrefs exact overview was requested for 16 service/location terms, followed by four matching-term seeds: window tinting Detroit, window film Detroit, window tinting Grand Rapids, and window film Grand Rapids. Each expansion was limited to 15 rows with only keyword and volume fields. Returned variants were reviewed for service fit; mixed automotive/general tinting queries were not used as architectural demand.

Four US/English desktop searches were requested for Detroit or Grand Rapids, but semantic QA found three organic-result payloads unrelated to the full requested query. They are excluded from ranking and competitor conclusions. The remaining Grand Rapids commercial response includes both relevant architectural businesses and automotive results; it is discovery context only, not a ranking baseline. See [SERP quality review](evidence/keyword-serp-quality-review.md).

Total actual Ahrefs cost for the entire Google/local/keyword sub-audit: **788 units**, below the 1,500-unit cap. Breakdown: DR 50; backlink statistics 50; exact keyword overview 126; existing audit issues 50; existing audit pages 50; matching-term expansions 165 + 55 + 165 + 77. No new vendor crawl was started.

Sources: [exact keywords](evidence/ahrefs-keywords-exact.json), [reviewed variants](evidence/keyword-variants-reviewed.json), `evidence/ahrefs-keyword-variants-1.json` through `4.json`, `evidence/keyword-serp-1.json` through `4.json`.

## What the site already earns

During 31 August–29 September, the homepage received 92 GSC clicks while Residential received one and Commercial zero. The Grand Rapids page received 10 clicks and 1,665 impressions, demonstrating an existing local discovery path even where Ahrefs reports very low phrase volume. These page totals are not phrase-specific attribution.

No rank-absence claim is supported by these four SERP responses. Detroit commercial returned dictionary/typography/whaling content; Detroit residential returned results about “MI,” including Xiaomi and a recipe; Grand Rapids residential returned generic tinting definitions, automotive laws and nonlocal results. Grand Rapids commercial has relevant architectural entries mixed with automotive results, but does not rescue the invalid comparisons. Use the verified GSC query/page evidence and a separately validated rank-tracking set for a baseline. No paid refresh was performed.

## Recommended sequence

1. Repair the verified user-path/content defects in the fresh audit, especially residential versus commercial navigation and local-page contact clarity.
2. Assign one principal page to each transactional cluster: residential, commercial, decorative and security. Inventory existing 3M/Detroit articles and service pages before creating content so older posts support rather than duplicate the chosen owner.
3. Add relevant internal links from educational posts and case studies to their service owner; keep educational pages useful for their informational intent.
4. Strengthen Grand Rapids with verified local projects, contact details and service pathways. Confirm any other city expansion against real coverage and project evidence.
5. Establish successful enquiry and qualified-lead measurement before interpreting traffic as business results. See [Google audit](GOOGLE-AUDIT.md).
