## Content improvements with useful evidence

1. Give selected real project examples an attributable source: confirmed project location/type, installed film system, approved image and supportable result. Link the example to its detail page only when that detail actually exists.
2. For technical guides, make author/company responsibility clear and link to relevant current manufacturer documentation when a reader needs specifications, installation limitations or warranties. Do not invent staff credentials or make all articles appear to be written by an unnamed expert.
3. Keep answers specific to the actual question. Educational heat/privacy/removal guides need not repeat every sales proposition. Use a natural relevant service link when useful; do not manufacture a formulaic FAQ for every page.
4. Align schema with corrected visible business/page content after the technical schema audit. Absence of a particular schema type is not, by itself, evidence that AI systems cannot use a page.
