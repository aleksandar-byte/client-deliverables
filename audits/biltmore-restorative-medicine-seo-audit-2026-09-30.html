01

The Asheville page shows two phone numbers

The local contact card says (828) 782-5245, while the same page’s footer and the main site say (828) 505-2885, leaving visitors with conflicting contact details.

  • Confirm whether either number is intentional call tracking and test where both calls route.
  • Use the confirmed public number consistently across the page, site and location data.
02

An empty Shopify page is in the sitemap

The indexable “Home page” collection is listed in the XML sitemap but displays “No Products Found,” adding a low-value page to the site’s search inventory.

  • Check whether the collection is used; if not, remove or noindex it through Shopify.
  • Confirm the generated sitemap no longer lists the unused page.
03

Business schema contains placeholder details

The homepage’s Organization data uses a Shopify “no-image” logo and six blank social-profile entries, so its machine-readable business identity is incomplete.

  • Use the real logo URL and remove empty social links.
  • After confirming each office’s phone and hours, add accurate location-specific clinic data.
04

Health articles lack clear bylines

Sampled health articles show dates but no named author or medical reviewer at the article opening, making editorial responsibility harder to see.

  • Confirm the actual writer and reviewer for each article before adding names or roles.
  • Show the verified credit near the title and link a clinician reviewer to the doctor bio where applicable.
05

Robots file points two crawlers to another site

The AhrefsBot and AhrefsSiteAudit sections of robots.txt each reference aimforwellbeing.org’s sitemap, although the main sitemap correctly points to Biltmore.

  • Remove or correct the two stale sitemap lines in the robots template.
  • Recheck robots.txt after publishing; this is not a general Google crawl block.

Fix order

Start with the contact conflict and pages or data that give search engines an inaccurate picture of the practice.

  • First: confirm Asheville’s phone, then clean the empty collection and structured data.
  • Next: correct robots.txt and add verified editorial credits to health articles.

This is a read-only, sampled audit. Google Business Profiles, Search Console, analytics and real-user speed data were not verified; no overall SEO score or Google penalty is inferred.
