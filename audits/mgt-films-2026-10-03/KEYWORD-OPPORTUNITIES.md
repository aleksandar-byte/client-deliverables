## SERP semantic quality check

Four desktopUS/English service/local queries were requested. Each saved response was read for actual topical/location relevance rather than trusting the query echo.

1. **Chicago commercial:** results include architectural specialists American Window Film, Window Film Chicago and Tinting Chicago. MGT's [affordable commercial article](https://mgtfilms.com/blog/affordable-commercial-window-tinting-chicago/) appears at organic position7 in this one response. This is a point-in-time result, not a sustained ranking. Review how that article connects to the local/service owners rather than assuming the Chicago landing page ranks for the phrase.
2. **Chicago residential:** mixed home-tint directory/provider results and automotive forums. Excluded from an architectural rank-absence claim.
3. **Milwaukee commercial:** heavily automotive/directory/social results, insufficiently aligned to commercial architectural intent. Excluded from ranking conclusions.
4. **Madison residential:** distant directory pages, social posts and auto-tint results. Excluded from a reliable local ranking baseline.

No claim that MGT is absent from the genuine results is made for the last three. No paid SERP refresh was performed. Sources: `evidence/keyword-serp-1.json` through`4.json`.
