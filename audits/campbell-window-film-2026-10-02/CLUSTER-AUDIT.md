## Overlap to review, not merge by default

The one-way privacy education page, daytime privacy installation page and [privacy film guide](https://campbellwindowfilm.com/privacy-window-film/) share topics but may serve different decisions. Page-level GSC shows 202 clicks on the first and 30 on the privacy guide for Aug 31–Sep 29. Retain both while checking their query sets and content roles. Page totals cannot establish that they compete on the same searches.

The existing [pros/cons guide](https://campbellwindowfilm.com/the-pros-and-cons-of-tinted-house-windows/) generated 199 clicks in that window. Its educational intent complements a residential installation hub. Do not reframe it as a sales page simply because traffic is broad. Determine whether visitors reach relevant service content and generate qualified enquiries before deciding on expansion.

## Wider context Nick should receive

The traffic opportunity appears concentrated in already-discovered informational pages, whereas the sampled commercial service page recorded 1 click / 755 impressions. That contrast makes clear journeys from helpful answers into the appropriate installation offer worth examining. It does not prove that informational traffic is poor quality or that more sales language will improve results. Use country/device/query and lead evidence from the Google/measurement module for a commercial decision.

A separate future cluster-planning exercise should use the exact market and service priorities, record top-10 organic URL sets for each query, calculate overlap, and compare GSC query/page ownership. This audit did not invent overlap counts, keyword volumes or an interactive cluster map unsupported by those measurements. Existing source limitations do not justify mass deletion, templated city expansion or a blanket cannibalization diagnosis.
