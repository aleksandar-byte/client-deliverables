Friendly Dental Group SEO Review

Priority findings and specific corrections for the current website

01

Location pages gained visibility, but the net result was negative

High

The location-page work produced measurable gains, but those gains did not offset the homepage decline. Ahrefs estimates that the 25 location pages gained 121 monthly organic visits while the homepage lost 605.

AreaPrevious estimateCurrent estimateNet change
Homepage1,353748-605 (-44.7%)
25 location pages combined2,5252,646+121 (+4.8%)
Homepage and location pages combined3,8783,394-484 (-12.5%)

The strongest location pages added 304 estimated visits, while declining offices lost 183. The resulting 121-visit location gain replaced only about 20% of the homepage loss.

Issues on the locational pages

  • The long treatment content is substantially duplicated across the location pages, with office names and cities substituted. Each office needs meaningful differentiation through its verified services, providers, patient logistics and local proof.
  • The location pages mention key treatments without consistently linking to the authoritative service pages.
  • Provider and office entities are not consistently connected. Provider profiles should identify and link to the offices where they practice, and location pages should link back to the relevant provider profiles.

Missing crucial internal links

The examples below show links that should be added within the Durham location page. Service mentions should link to the matching service pages. The provider name should link to the provider bio, and the provider bio should link back to the Durham office page.

Critical

The total includes seven referring domains pointing to the Fort Mill URL, so the true backlink count may be higher. Two additional legacy URLs currently show no detected backlinks but should still be resolved during the same redirect cleanup.

Critical

The Charlotte-Whitehall GBP website button points to Fiverr instead of the practice website.

These are high-priority local SEO issues. Nine public profiles were checked. Their addresses and phone numbers match the corresponding location pages, but website links, hours, booking links and profile information are inconsistent. These errors can affect local visibility, user trust and appointment conversion.
