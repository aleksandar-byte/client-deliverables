## Architecture direction

Keep three useful dimensions clear: **product/system**, **audience/application**, and **served location**. An educational question is a fourth supporting content type, not automatically a competing sales page. Fix the current route/copy defects before expanding the already broad city-by-service library.

## Linking plan

1. Keep the primary menu focused on Residential, Commercial, product solutions, Locations, About, Blog and Contact; these routes already exist. Do not create a second redundant navigation system.
2. Fix the specific wrong security-card destinations on Commercial before adding more links.
3. From useful education, link naturally to the relevant installation/product route and an appropriate location/quote option. Audit the actual body first: the Tulsa healthcare article already contains solar, decorative, resurfacing and related-article links.
4. Product pages should link to appropriate audience/city routes and one or two genuinely useful guides. Replace placeholder Related Services links when confirmed in DOM.
5. Location pages should connect to their real services and local proof. A series of Learn More cards should open details; if all go to Contact, relabel as quote actions or restore detail routes.
