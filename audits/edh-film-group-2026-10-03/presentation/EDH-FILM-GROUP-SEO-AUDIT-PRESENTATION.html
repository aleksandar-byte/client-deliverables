The hero image starts loading late

The mobile lab scored 31/100 with a 39.14-second simulated LCP; the hero background is discovered late while all five slides load together.

  • Make the first hero slide discoverable early and prioritize its responsive image; defer inactive slides.
  • Load the below-fold Vimeo player later, then retest the hero and contact functions.
02

The team section is still a template

The About page shows six placeholder team profiles and seven Lorem ipsum blocks instead of real staff information.

  • Replace the section with client-confirmed names, photos and short bios, or remove it until those are available.
03

Security buttons open the wrong product

The commercial page’s S800, S1400, S2400 and Riot Glass buttons all open Solar Window Film.

  • Point S800, S1400 and S2400 to Security Window Film, and Riot Glass to Security Glass.
  • Edit these four links directly; do not redirect the valid solar product page.
04

Tulsa sends visitors to Oklahoma City

The Tulsa location card repeats the Oklahoma City address and opens an Oklahoma City map pin.

  • Confirm the real Tulsa office address and map with EDH, then correct that card.
05

A useful guide contains editing leftovers

The Low-E window-film guide includes an editing instruction and a cut-off warranty sentence despite earning 35 Google clicks in the review period.

  • Remove the editing instruction and complete the warranty sentence after checking the wording.
06

Phone clicks miss conversion credit

GA4 records phone_number_link_click, but the configured phone key event is phone_click, so 104 recorded clicks receive no phone key-event credit.

  • Verify the tracking setup, align the actual phone event with the key-event setting and test it.
  • Keep click counts separate from completed calls and qualified leads.

Fix order

Repair visitor routes and unfinished copy first, then improve hero loading and measurement.

  • First: security links, Tulsa confirmation, team placeholders and the Low-E guide.
  • Next: first-slide loading, deferred video and verified phone-event mapping.
