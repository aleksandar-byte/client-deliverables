ElementNew proposal

Basement and deckNew 8.60×3.75m basement footprint: 32.25m² outside, 26.33m² inside assumed 250mm walls, including stairs. Approximately 11.25m² of deck overlaps its roof. Waterproof structural roof required beneath timber.

Still to resolve before a final design

Concrete supports and beam depths; roof structure and insulation; stair/office fire separation; waterproofing, drainage and ventilation; occupied dining-chair/appliance clearances; local planning limits, area measurement and budget. The new roof is a significant whole-house change. No claim is made that it meets the 151m² cap or costs less.

Headroom and area figures are concept geometry, not certified construction dimensions. The existing 3D still shows the superseded raised office and must not be mistaken for this proposal.
