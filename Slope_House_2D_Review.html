Review findings and revised proposal

The earlier draft had a missing office doorway, no adequate office bottom landing, two separate flights, an oversized unconfirmed deck and misleading free-basement-space implications. This revision uses one U-shaped core in the left living/dining zone, aligned on all levels. The office is now above that zone, not the primary bedroom.

Basement: 27.0 m² inside walls, including a 9.1 m² stair envelope; approximately 17.8 m² remains outside that envelope. Office: 15.1 m² clear; whole upper footprint with stair and walls approximately 30.3 m². Deck study reduced from 71.5 to 42.1 m².

The dashed island is an unresolved fit, not an approved arrangement. Stair headroom, roof and structure, guarding/glazing, basement waterproofing/daylight and site grading require further design. The old 151 m² cap is exceeded. No changes to the current 3D model.
