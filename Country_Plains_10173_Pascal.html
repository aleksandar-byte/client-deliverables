Layout changes · office trade-offs

Kids’ desks face the outside wall, with chairs pulling into the centre of each room. The 120 × 200 cm beds and recessed wardrobes remain. Island stools are removed; dining seating stays. The right-side run follows the original drawing: corner pantry, short counter, then fridge nearest dining, facing the island. Kitchen design and references

Muted olive cabinets, warm stone worktops, the oak island and restrained brushed-metal details. Matching appliance panels, consistent worktop heights, recessed plinths and a quieter splashback make the kitchen read as one fitted design. The oven glass remains exposed.

Appliance integration follows Bosch’s integrated-dishwasher guidance; coordinated fronts and fittings are informed by Blum’s cabinet-front guidance. The palette is our design proposal. This is a visual concept: final panel-ready appliances, ventilation and installation clearances require product selection.
