What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a small post placed in the jaw to support a replacement tooth. An abutment connects the post to a crown, bridge, or other restoration above the gumline.

The right plan depends on how many teeth are missing, the condition of your gums and bone, your general health, and the type of restoration you need. Implants also require ongoing brushing, cleaning, and professional care.

1

Implant post

The part positioned in the jaw to support the restoration.

2

Abutment

The connector between the implant post and the replacement tooth.

3

Restoration

The visible crown, bridge, or prosthesis planned for your needs.
