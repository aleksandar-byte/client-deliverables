Common questions

Before your visit.

Is the Brighton office actually in Allston?

Yes. The office serves Brighton, Allston, and nearby neighborhoods from 599 Cambridge Street in Allston, Massachusetts.

What kinds of dental care are available?

The practice offers preventive, restorative, cosmetic, implant, periodontal, Invisalign, root canal, laser, sleep apnea, and other dental care pathways. Call to confirm the right appointment for your needs.

Do you accept dental insurance?

The practice states that it accepts most insurance plans. Because benefits vary, contact the office and your insurance carrier to confirm coverage before treatment.

What should I do about an urgent dental problem?

Call the Brighton office at (617) 782-9250 for guidance. If you have a life-threatening emergency, call 911 or seek emergency medical care.

Can I send medical information through the website form?

No. The current website asks patients to use its form only for non-medical questions and correspondence. Call the office for clinical concerns.
