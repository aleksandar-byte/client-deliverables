Technology at this office

Digital tools supporting care for Delray Beach patients

Delray Beach patients visiting the Boynton Beach office have access to digital imaging, scanning, magnification and in-house resources when appropriate for their care.

Digital imaging and CBCT

Panoramic, cephalometric and CBCT 3D imaging can help the dentist assess anatomy for applicable care, including dental implant planning.

iTero digital scanning

iTero scanning supports impression-free digital scans and may be used when planning Invisalign treatment.

Dental microscope

A dental microscope gives the dentist a magnified view when it is useful during applicable general dentistry procedures.

In-house lab

The practice lists an in-house lab among the resources available to support applicable general dentistry care.

Before your visit

Delray Beach patient questions

Where do Delray Beach patients visit Cap Dental?

Patients from Delray Beach visit Cap Dental's Boynton Beach office at 2300 S Congress Ave, Suite 110. Patients traveling from the Treasure Coast can also visit the Port St. Lucie office.

Is parking available?

The Boynton Beach location offers free onsite parking for patients arriving from Delray Beach.

What dental services are available?

Delray Beach patients can access general, family, emergency, implant, Invisalign and cosmetic dentistry at the Boynton Beach office. Recommendations depend on an individual examination.

Does Cap Dental accept insurance?

Cap Dental lists most PPO insurance plans. Please call to verify participation and plan-specific benefits.

What happens at a first visit?

A first appointment generally includes an evaluation, any necessary digital X-rays and time to discuss your concerns with the dentist.

How do I schedule from Delray Beach?

Delray Beach patients can call (561) 560-8787 or submit an appointment request online.
