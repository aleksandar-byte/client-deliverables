Technology at this office

Digital tools supporting care in Boynton Beach

The Boynton Beach team uses digital imaging, scanning, magnification and an in-house lab to support diagnosis and treatment planning.

Digital imaging and CBCT

Panoramic, cephalometric and 3D CBCT images give the dentist detailed views for treatment planning, including dental implants.

iTero digital scanning

iTero creates digital scans without traditional impressions and can support Invisalign treatment planning.

Dental microscope

A dental microscope provides a magnified view during general dentistry procedures that benefit from a closer look.

In-house lab

The in-house lab supports the team during select general dentistry procedures.

Before your visit

Boynton Beach office questions

Where is Cap Dental in Boynton Beach?

You can find Cap Dental's Boynton Beach office at 2300 S Congress Ave, Suite 110, near Boynton Beach Mall and Quantum Town Center. Cap Dental also has a Port St. Lucie office for patients traveling from the Treasure Coast.

Is parking available?

Yes. Free onsite parking is available at the shared healthcare building.

What dental services are available?

The Boynton Beach office offers general, family, emergency, implant, Invisalign and cosmetic dentistry. Your dentist will recommend options based on your exam and individual needs.

Does Cap Dental accept insurance?

Cap Dental accepts many major PPO dental plans. Call the office to confirm participation and your plan's benefits.

What happens at a first visit?

Your first visit may include an exam, digital X-rays when needed and a conversation about your goals and treatment options.

How do I schedule at the Boynton Beach office?

Call (561) 560-8787 or request an appointment online with the Boynton Beach team.
