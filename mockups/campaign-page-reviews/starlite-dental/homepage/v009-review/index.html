A LITTLE CLARITY HELPS

Before your visit.

Here are answers to questions patients often ask.

Do you help patients who are nervous about the dentist?+

Yes. Tell us about your concerns when you book. Our comfort options include headphones, warm towels, blankets and aromatherapy. You can also discuss sedation options and suitability with the dentist.

Can you help me understand my dental insurance?+

Yes. Share your exact PPO plan with the team so we can help verify benefits and submit claims. Your coverage, network status and out-of-pocket costs depend on your individual plan.

What if I have tooth pain or a broken tooth?+

Call (214) 504-0500 and describe your symptoms. Our emergency dentistry page explains urgent dental concerns and the next step. For trouble breathing or swallowing, uncontrolled heavy bleeding or serious facial injury, call 911 or seek emergency medical care.

Can I visit without dental insurance?+

Yes. Ask the team about self-pay options, CareCredit financing and our dental discount plan. Financing is subject to eligibility, approval and terms.

Will all my treatment be completed at my first visit?+

Your first visit and any treatment depend on the reason for your appointment, the examination and available time. The dentist explains the findings and recommended next steps before you decide on care.
