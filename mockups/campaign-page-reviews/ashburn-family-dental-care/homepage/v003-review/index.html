Tools that support your care

Digital dentistry.
Practical benefits.

We use CEREC technology for chairside restorations and digital X-rays to assess teeth and supporting structures. The dentist recommends imaging and treatment based on your examination.

More about our digital tools +

Imaging and scans

Our 3D CT imaging supports assessment of complex cases. The iTero oral scanner captures images used to create digital models of your teeth.

Restorations made in-house

Our in-house printers and mills produce restorations such as crowns, fillings, bridges and veneers. CEREC can support single-visit restorations when appropriate for your treatment.
