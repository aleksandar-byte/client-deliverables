At your pace

Nervous about visiting the dentist?

If you're nervous about dental care or haven't visited in a while, tell us when you book. You're welcome to ask questions about the visit and the care we recommend.

Had a difficult experience before? Tell us what worried you and what would help you feel more comfortable. You can also ask about costs before agreeing to treatment.

Talk to our team

Smile Makeover

What would you like to change when you smile? Your teeth, bite and existing dental work help determine which treatments make sense. The options may include whitening, bonding, veneers, crowns or Invisalign.

If you already have a treatment plan, bring it with you. We can talk through your questions and the expected costs.

Explore Smile MakeoverSmile Gallery

Clear aligners

Invisalign

We'll check your teeth and bite to see whether Invisalign clear aligners are right for you. iTero scans can help us plan treatment. You'll need to wear the aligners every day and return for follow-up visits. Ask us how treatment could fit into your routine.

Explore Invisalign

Replace missing teeth

Dental Implants

Dental implants can support replacement teeth. If you're missing teeth, we can assess whether implants are an option for you. We may use CBCT images to help plan treatment.

Tell us if eating or speaking has become difficult. Ask what treatment would involve, including the cost and any later visits.

Explore dental implants

How we use 3D imaging and digital scans

CBCT 3D imaging

Cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) produces 3D dental images. We may use these images to assess the area when planning dental implant treatment.

About CBCT imaging

iTero digital scans

An iTero scan creates a 3D model of your teeth to help plan Invisalign treatment. The smile preview isn't a guarantee of how your teeth will look after treatment.

About iTero scanning

Questions before you book

Are you accepting new patients in Bethlehem?

Yes. New patients are welcome at our Bethlehem office. Book a visit online or call (610) 868-7601, and tell the team whether you need routine care or help with a specific problem.

Do you accept my dental insurance?

Call with your insurance details so the team can verify your benefits. Coverage, network status and your share depend on your plan. We submit claims as a courtesy. See our insurance and payment information.

How much will my first dental visit cost?

Call us for the price of the appointment you're booking, including any exam, cleaning or X-rays. Your insurance benefits may affect what you pay.

Can I book an emergency dental visit?

We provide emergency dentistry in Bethlehem for problems such as tooth pain and broken teeth. Call (610) 868-7601 before coming so the team can discuss the next step and appointment availability. See our emergency dental care guidance for what to expect and urgent warning signs.

Is your office open on Saturdays?

Yes. We open on the first and third Saturdays of the month, from 8:30 AM to 12:30 PM, and close on Sundays. Call (610) 868-7601 to check appointment availability.

What happens if I call after hours?

Call (610) 868-7601 and follow the current recorded instructions. Existing patients have a dental-emergency option; new patients have a separate prompt. The recording does not mean the office is open for treatment. See our emergency care guidance for urgent symptoms.

How do I change or cancel an appointment?

To confirm, change or cancel an existing appointment, call (610) 868-7601.
