Useful questions

Plan your visit

Which SoftTouch office should I contact?

Choose Brighton/Allston, Newtonville, or Brookline based on the address and published hours that work for you. Each location card provides that office's direct phone number and exact Google listing.

Can I receive specialty dental care at SoftTouch?

Yes. The published team includes providers for cosmetic dentistry, endodontic care, periodontics, and implant care. Available treatment also includes Invisalign, laser dentistry, sleep apnea appliances, dentures, and Botox for selected TMJ and migraine concerns. The team can confirm the appropriate provider and office for your needs.

Which communities does SoftTouch Dentistry serve?

SoftTouch has offices in Brighton/Allston, Newtonville, and Brookline and serves patients from Boston, Cambridge, Watertown, Wellesley, Weston, and nearby Greater Boston communities.

How can I prepare for an appointment request?

Choose an office, have your contact details and reason for visiting ready, then call or submit the online form. Confirm insurance, records, and visit preparation with the office.

What should I do for an urgent dental need?

Please call the practice. SoftTouch Dentistry makes a best effort to see urgent patients the same day, but same-day treatment is not guaranteed. Review the urgent dental care information.

How do I verify insurance and payment options?

The practice lists cash, checks, HSAs, major cards, CareCredit, and plans including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Delta Dental, Cigna, Guardian, Aetna, and MetLife. Contact the office and your plan to confirm current participation and expected costs, then review the patient information page.
