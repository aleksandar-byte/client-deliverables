Start with what you need help with

Some visits are preventive. Others begin with a damaged tooth, missing teeth, a cosmetic concern, dental anxiety, or a sleep problem. These pathways connect each concern with the most relevant care.

01

Keep your family on track

Plan exams, preventive visits, and ongoing family dental care for children, teens, adults, and seniors.

02

Repair or replace teeth

Explore ways to repair damaged teeth or replace missing teeth, including crowns, dentures, and dental implants.

03

Change something about your smile

If color, shape, spacing, or alignment bothers you, compare options to improve the appearance of your smile.

04

Make care easier to manage

Ask about options for dental anxiety or whether oral-appliance sleep apnea treatment may fit your situation.

Meet the dentists

Training that supports a wider range of care

Dr. Timothy Breeden and Dr. Nicole Chambers are general dentists whose backgrounds include two-year General Practice Residencies at the Central Virginia VA Medical Center.

Dr. Timothy Breeden, MS, DDS, d-ABDSM

Dr. Breeden's work includes restorative and implant cases, grafting, full-mouth care, TMJ therapy, and dental sleep medicine. He joined private practice in 2002 and assumed ownership in 2005.

Dr. Nicole Chambers, DDS

Dr. Chambers has advanced training in implants, crowns and bridges, pre-prosthetic surgery, grafting, IV and oral sedation, and periodontal care.

Common questions about visiting

Is an online appointment request immediately confirmed?

No. The request form lets you propose a visit, but the office will contact you to confirm the appointment.

Can a new patient call about a dental emergency?

Yes. The practice's emergency guidance welcomes calls from new and existing patients with tooth pain, swelling, broken teeth, lost restorations, or dental injuries.

Where is the office and when is it open?

The office is at 10601 Greenyard Way in Chester. Posted hours are Monday through Thursday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
