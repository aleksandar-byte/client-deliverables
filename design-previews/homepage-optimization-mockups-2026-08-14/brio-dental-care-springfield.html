Questions before an appointment

Good to know before you book

Is Brio Dental Care accepting new patients?

The practice has a dedicated new-patient process and online appointment request page. Contact the office to confirm current appointment availability and the type of visit you need.

Does the practice see children and adults?

Brio Dental Care states that it provides dental care for patients of all ages. When booking for a child, share their age and the reason for the visit so the team can confirm the right appointment.

Which dental insurance plans are accepted?

The website displays several insurance networks, but participation and benefits can change. Give the office your plan details before treatment so the team can verify current coverage.

What should I do if I have a dental emergency?

Call the office when you have significant tooth pain, swelling, trauma, or another urgent concern. The team can ask about your symptoms and advise how quickly you should be evaluated.

Can the team assist patients in languages other than English?

The practice states that its team speaks English, Spanish, Arabic, and Italian. Mention your preferred language when scheduling so the office can plan appropriately.
